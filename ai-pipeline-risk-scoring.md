# AI Pipeline Risk Scoring

## Purpose

Not all opportunities have the same probability of closing.

AI helps revenue teams identify deal risk early and focus attention where it matters most.

---

## Why Risk Scoring Matters

Revenue teams often discover deal risk too late.

Common consequences:

- Missed forecasts
- Deal slippage
- Poor resource allocation
- Revenue surprises

AI helps surface risk signals before they impact outcomes.

---

## Risk Categories

### Low Risk

Characteristics:

- Decision maker engaged
- Business pain validated
- Budget confirmed
- Next steps defined

Probability:

80-100%

---

### Medium Risk

Characteristics:

- Some qualification completed
- Limited stakeholder engagement
- Moderate activity levels

Probability:

50-79%

---

### High Risk

Characteristics:

- No recent activity
- Missing next steps
- Weak qualification
- Long deal aging

Probability:

Below 50%

---

## AI Risk Signals

### Engagement Signals

Evaluate:

- Email replies
- Meeting attendance
- Website activity
- Stakeholder participation

---

### Pipeline Signals

Evaluate:

- Stage progression
- Opportunity aging
- Close date movement
- Activity frequency

---

### Qualification Signals

Evaluate:

- Budget confirmed
- Authority identified
- Need validated
- Timeline established

---

## Deal Health Score

### Components

- Fit Score
- Intent Score
- Engagement Score
- Qualification Score

### Output

Overall Risk Rating

- Green
- Yellow
- Red

---

## Recommended Actions

### Green

- Continue progression
- Maintain engagement

### Yellow

- Review qualification
- Increase stakeholder engagement

### Red

- Escalate review
- Requalify opportunity
- Adjust forecast

---

## Benefits

- Improved forecast accuracy
- Better pipeline visibility
- Faster intervention
- Higher win rates

---

## Key Principle

Revenue risk should never be a surprise.

AI helps identify risk before it becomes a missed forecast.
