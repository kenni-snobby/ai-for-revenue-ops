# AI CRM Cleanup Workflow

## Purpose

CRM quality directly impacts forecasting, reporting, and sales productivity.

AI can automate CRM hygiene and improve data accuracy at scale.

---

## Common CRM Problems

- Duplicate records
- Missing fields
- Inconsistent naming
- Outdated contact information
- Incorrect lifecycle stages

---

## AI Cleanup Process

### Step 1: Identify Duplicates

AI analyzes:

- Email addresses
- Company names
- Contact names
- Phone numbers

Action:

Merge duplicate records.

---

### Step 2: Detect Missing Data

AI identifies:

- Missing job titles
- Missing industries
- Missing company details
- Missing lead sources

Action:

Enrich records automatically.

---

### Step 3: Validate Data Quality

AI reviews:

- Invalid emails
- Broken URLs
- Incomplete records
- Outdated information

Action:

Flag records for review.

---

### Step 4: Standardize Data

AI normalizes:

- Company names
- Industries
- Territories
- Job titles

Action:

Improve reporting consistency.

---

### Step 5: Lifecycle Stage Validation

AI checks:

- Engagement activity
- Opportunity history
- Customer status

Action:

Recommend lifecycle updates.

---

## Benefits

- Better forecasting
- Cleaner reporting
- Faster sales execution
- Improved automation

---

## Recommended Review Cadence

### Weekly

- New duplicates
- Missing fields

### Monthly

- Data quality review
- Lifecycle audit

### Quarterly

- Full CRM audit

---

## Key Principle

A CRM is only as valuable as the quality of its data.

AI helps maintain trust in that data.
