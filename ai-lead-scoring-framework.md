# AI Lead Scoring Framework

## Purpose

Lead scoring helps revenue teams prioritize the highest-value opportunities.

AI improves lead scoring by analyzing patterns, engagement signals, and historical outcomes.

---

## Traditional Lead Scoring

Typically based on:

- Job Title
- Company Size
- Industry
- Geography

Limitation:

Static scoring often ignores intent.

---

## AI-Powered Lead Scoring

AI evaluates:

- Website behavior
- Email engagement
- Content consumption
- Meeting activity
- Historical conversion patterns

---

## Scoring Categories

### Fit Score

Measures ICP alignment.

Factors:

- Industry
- Company Size
- Revenue
- Technology Stack

---

### Intent Score

Measures buying interest.

Factors:

- Pricing page visits
- Demo requests
- Repeat visits
- Content engagement

---

### Engagement Score

Measures interaction level.

Factors:

- Email opens
- Email replies
- Meeting attendance
- Website activity

---

## Lead Prioritization

### Hot Leads

Score: 80-100

Action:

Immediate sales outreach.

---

### Warm Leads

Score: 60-79

Action:

Nurture and monitor.

---

### Cold Leads

Score: Below 60

Action:

Automated nurture workflows.

---

## Benefits

- Better prioritization
- Higher conversion rates
- Faster response times
- Improved forecasting

---

## Key Principle

Not all leads deserve equal attention.

AI helps identify where attention creates the highest return.
