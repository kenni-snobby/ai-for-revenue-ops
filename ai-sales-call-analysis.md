# AI Sales Call Analysis

## Purpose

Sales calls contain valuable insights.

AI helps identify patterns, coaching opportunities, and deal risks at scale.

---

## Why Analyze Calls

Traditional call reviews are:

- Time-consuming
- Inconsistent
- Difficult to scale

AI enables faster and more objective analysis.

---

## Key Areas AI Reviews

### Discovery Quality

Questions:

- Were pain points identified?
- Were business goals discussed?
- Was urgency explored?

---

### Qualification Quality

Evaluate:

- Budget discussed
- Authority identified
- Need validated
- Timeline confirmed

---

### Objection Handling

Analyze:

- Objections raised
- Response quality
- Resolution effectiveness

---

### Engagement Signals

Measure:

- Talk-to-listen ratio
- Customer participation
- Question quality
- Meeting engagement

---

### Deal Risk Indicators

Warning signs:

- No clear next steps
- No decision maker involvement
- Weak business case
- Low urgency

---

## Coaching Scorecard

Evaluate:

### Discovery

Score: 1–10

### Qualification

Score: 1–10

### Communication

Score: 1–10

### Objection Handling

Score: 1–10

### Closing Skills

Score: 1–10

---

## Benefits

- Faster coaching
- Consistent reviews
- Better qualification
- Improved win rates

---

## Recommended Workflow

1. Record call
2. Transcribe call
3. Analyze with AI
4. Generate coaching insights
5. Track improvement over time

---

## Key Principle

Great coaching scales revenue.

AI helps coaching scale.
